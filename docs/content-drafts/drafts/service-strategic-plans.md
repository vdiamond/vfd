## Title

Strategic plans that deliver measurable value

## Summary (card)

Co-create IT strategy with the business — plans you will use, with outcomes you can track.

## Body

### What this is

We sit with your leaders and build strategic plans that connect IT choices to business value. Not a binder for the shelf. A living plan with priorities, ownership, and measures that matter.

### Who it is for

Government of Canada IT departments missing a clear strategy — or holding one that no longer matches budget, risk, or mandate. Also useful when architecture, delivery, and business priorities have drifted apart.

### What we focus on

- Priorities tied to measurable value  
- Trade-offs made explicit (cost, risk, time, service)  
- Alignment between IT and business interaction  
- A sequence of work your organization can actually absorb  

### Co-creation, not hand-off

You know your department. We bring structure, challenge, and delivery realism. The plan is yours — we help you make it honest and usable.

### How it connects

Often follows a readiness assessment, or sits alongside cost-savings and operating-model work. See [Approach](/approach) for engagement models. Pricing **TBD / unpublished**.
