## Title

Digital readiness assessments

## Summary (card)

Know what you can take on before you commit — so change does not break service.

## Body

### What this is

A clear-eyed assessment of whether your organization is ready to adopt a technology, operating change, or delivery approach — and what must be true first.

We look at capability, constraints, risk, and the conditions for success. You get a frank read, not a sales pitch for the next shiny thing.

### Who it is for

IT Directors and CIO equivalents in the Government of Canada who need to move forward carefully: new platforms, modernization, vendor shifts, or structural change under budget and service pressure.

### What you walk away with

- A shared picture of readiness across the areas that matter  
- Gaps ranked by impact on delivery and service  
- Practical next steps you can act on with your team  
- A basis for strategy, investment, or a go / no-go call  

### How it connects

Often the front door to strategic planning, project remediation, or cost work. Engagement can be per diem or, where results are measurable, structured around outcomes — see [Approach](/approach). Pricing **TBD / unpublished**.
