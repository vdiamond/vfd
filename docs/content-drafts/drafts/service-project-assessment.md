## Title

Project assessment and remediation

## Summary (card)

An honest read on projects at risk — then a practical path to get delivery back under control.

## Body

### What this is

When a project is late, over budget, or unclear, you need facts before more spend. We assess scope, governance, delivery practice, dependencies, and whether the plan still matches reality.

Where recovery is possible, we help shape remediation: what to stop, what to fix, and what to renegotiate with the business.

### Who it is for

GoC IT leaders accountable for projects that must land on time and on budget — or that need a clear recommendation to re-baseline or stop.

### What you walk away with

- A plain-language assessment of status and root causes  
- Options ranked by realism (recover, re-scope, pause)  
- A remediation outline your team and vendors can execute  
- Clearer decision points for executives  

### Stance

We are direct. We do not dress up a failing trajectory. Skin in the game means we care whether the recovery actually works — see [Approach](/approach). Pricing **TBD / unpublished**.
