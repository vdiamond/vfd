## Model 1 — Consultants on a per diem

Typically one consultant working with your team. You get senior attention on the file: readiness, strategy, project recovery, cost, operating model, or architecture.

**Fits when:**

- You need embedded expertise for a defined period  
- Scope is exploratory or evolving  
- Procurement prefers time-and-materials style arrangements

## Model 2 — Pay per real results realized

Where outcomes can be measured, we can align fees to results — for example, a percentage of real cost savings realized over one year.

**Fits when:**

- Baseline and measurement are credible  
- Both parties can define “real” savings (not paper cuts)  
- You want a partner with skin in the game
