## Title

IT team structures and business interaction

## Summary (card)

Design teams and working rhythms that partner with the business — clear ownership, less friction.

## Body

### What this is

Help defining how IT is organized and how it shows up with business partners: roles, decision rights, intake, prioritization, and the day-to-day interaction that makes strategy executable.

### Who it is for

GoC IT leaders dealing with org friction — IT stuck as an order-taker, unclear ownership, or structures that cannot absorb change while keeping service stable.

### What good looks like

- Structures matched to mandate and capacity  
- Business interaction that is predictable and respectful of both sides  
- Room to reduce duplication without hollowed-out capability  
- A model your directors can explain in one page
