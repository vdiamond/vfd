### What this is

Practical identification and delivery of IT cost-savings measures: spend, tooling, vendor arrangements, process waste, and structural choices that no longer earn their keep.

The goal is efficiency you can defend — lower cost while maintaining service levels, including where headcount pressure is real.

### Who it is for

IT Directors and CIO equivalents facing budget constraint who still own uptime, compliance, and delivery.

### How we work it

- Baseline what you actually spend and why  
- Separate one-time cuts from sustainable change  
- Protect service and risk posture while reducing waste  
- Track savings that are real — not paper optimism
