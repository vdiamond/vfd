## Title

Enterprise architecture closer to the business

## Summary (card)

Reposition EA so it enables decisions and delivery — not gatekeeping for its own sake.

## Body

### The problem

Enterprise architecture often sits too far from the business. It becomes a review bottleneck, a parallel language, or a set of diagrams nobody uses when priorities shift.

### What we do

We help you place EA where it earns trust: closer to business outcomes, clearer on trade-offs, and useful to delivery teams. Architecture becomes a decision aid — standards with purpose, roadmaps tied to value, and advice that lands in time to matter.

### Who it is for

CIO-level and senior IT leaders who need architecture to support time-to-market, legacy modernization, and safe adoption of new technology — without slowing every file to a crawl.

### Outcomes we aim for

- EA engaged early on initiatives that matter  
- Fewer late surprises on integration, risk, and cost  
- Shared vocabulary between business and IT  
- Architecture work prioritized by value, not volume
