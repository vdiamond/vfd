## Who we are

Value From Digital Inc. (“**Value From Digital**,” “**we**,” “**us**”) operates https://valuefromdigital.com. This policy describes how we handle personal information in connection with this website and related contact channels.

## Information we collect

We may collect personal information you choose to give us when you contact us, including:

- Name and contact details (for example email address)
- Message content and any attachments you send
- Basic technical data our hosting environment may log (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested)

We do not require an account to browse the public site. If you contact us via LinkedIn, LinkedIn’s own privacy policy also applies to that platform.

## How we use information

We use this information to:

- Respond to inquiries and manage engagements
- Operate, secure, and improve the website
- Meet legal or professional obligations when required

We do not sell personal information.

## Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us host or operate the site (for example hosting and email), only as needed for those services, or when the law requires disclosure.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The site may use cookies or similar technologies needed for basic operation and security (for example session or preference cookies). You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law.
